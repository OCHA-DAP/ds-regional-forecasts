African Regional Seasonal Forecasts

Archive of seasonal forecast products from ACMAD (continental Long-Range Forecasts, ACCOF statements, RCOF outputs), AGRHYMET (PRESASS & PRESAGG forums), and the SADC Climate Services Centre (SARCOF forum statements back to 2002 and objective seasonal forecast maps), assembled to compare regional consensus products against global forecasts such as ECMWF SEAS5. Sources: ACMAD THREDDS server, agrhymet.cilss.int, sadc.int, csc.sadc.int, the Wayback Machine, and the WAS-NextGen digitized dataset (Zenodo, CC-BY 4.0).
