African Regional Seasonal Forecasts

Archive of seasonal forecast products from ACMAD (continental Long-Range Forecasts, ACCOF statements, RCOF outputs) and AGRHYMET (PRESASS & PRESAGG forums), assembled to compare regional consensus products against global forecasts such as ECMWF SEAS5. Sources: ACMAD THREDDS server, agrhymet.cilss.int, the Wayback Machine, and the WAS-NextGen digitized dataset (Zenodo, CC-BY 4.0).
